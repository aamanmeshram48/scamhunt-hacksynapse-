# ScamHunt 

### AI-Powered Scam Detection & Cyber Safety Platform
ScamHunt is an AI-powered cybersecurity platform that helps users **detect, understand and respond to digital threats**.

It can analyze suspicious **messages, URLs, screenshots and files** and provide a risk score, threat category, explanation and recommended action.
---

##  Problem

Online scams are becoming more common and difficult to identify.

Users may receive:

- Phishing links
- Fake KYC or banking messages
- Job scams
- Shopping scams
- Impersonation attempts
- Suspicious files and screenshots
- Harassment or cyberbullying content
  
Many users are not sure whether the content is safe or what they should do after an incident.

---
